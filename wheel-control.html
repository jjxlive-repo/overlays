Spin The Screen

Operator controls — setup only, not a browser source.

Tier floors

In coin equivalents — 1 TikTok coin = 1 YouTube jewel = $0.01 of viewer spend, and a Super Chat converts at the same rate. Floors decide which TIER a wheel gift's reel lands on; they must be strictly increasing. The six wheel gifts below always spin — one under the spin floor still qualifies at NORMAL — while any other gift never spins at all.

Wheel gifts

v2 mechanic: each gift is hard-mapped to its own effect — nothing is rolled. The reel spins for the tier only. Unticking an effect is a kill switch: its gift then produces no spin at all (never a different effect). At least one must stay on. Freeze & Shatter and Ball Drop use the Gesture Engine's captured stream; keep that window open for their full versions. Both fall back safely when tracking is unavailable.

Tier reel

The reel that lands on NORMAL / RARE / EPIC / LEGENDARY before the effect plays. Style switches between the carnival wheel and the slot card. Offsets move its centre off the default (540, 960) — the exact middle of the frame; scale resizes it in place. Changes apply to the next spin live — use the tester below to see them — and persist on Save.

Effect longevity

How long each tier's effect HOLDS on screen, in milliseconds — the dwell between the impact landing and the effect clearing. This is the main dial for how much a spin interrupts you: the same effect reads as a flourish or as a takeover purely on duration. Applied when a spin is RESOLVED, so a spin always plays with the timing that was set when it fired.

Ball Drop lens

The ball carries a warped crop of your camera rather than a flat one, so the face fills the sphere instead of sitting small in the middle of the room. Power is how hard the centre bulges — 1.0 is no distortion, higher pushes the nose into the glass. Crop is how much around your head to take: lower is tighter. Both need head tracking, since the crop centres on the tracked face.

Signature lifetimes

How long a name stays on the signature registry. Takeover and Legend are session-scoped and have no clock — they clear when the dock's session rotates. Note the supporter wall deliberately ignores all of this: bricks are permanent until the wall wraps, because a brick cannot vanish out of the middle of a physical wall.

Wheel tester

Pick a gift — its effect is fixed by the map, so the selects mirror production exactly. Test full spin plays the tier reel, then the gift's effect. Preview effect only skips the reel. Neither lays a brick or persists, but both appear on the live overlay.

Live from the dock. The wall rebuilds from this on every overlay reload.
