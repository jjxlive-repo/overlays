Spin The Screen

Operator controls — setup only, not a browser source.

Tier floors

In coin equivalents — 1 TikTok coin = 1 YouTube jewel = $0.01 of viewer spend, and a Super Chat converts at the same rate. A gift below the spin floor produces no spin at all: that floor doubles as the qualification threshold. Floors must be strictly increasing.

Enabled results

Disabled results are never selected and never shown; probability redistributes equally among the rest. At least one must stay on. With exactly one enabled the overlay skips the spin animation — a reel that can only land on one slice is a lie.

Effect longevity

How long each tier's effect HOLDS on screen, in milliseconds — the dwell between the impact landing and the effect clearing. This is the main dial for how much a spin interrupts you: the same effect reads as a flourish or as a takeover purely on duration. Applied when a spin is RESOLVED, so a spin always plays with the timing that was set when it fired.

Reel size & position

The spin reel defaults to a large centred frame. Scale shrinks or grows the whole housing; X/Y nudge it off-centre in pixels (positive X is right, positive Y is down). Content scale is separate from Scale — it resizes just the icon and text inside, so you can shrink the housing to tuck it out of the way and grow the content back up to stay readable, instead of both shrinking together. Changes here apply to the live overlay immediately as you type — this is the one section that previews itself, no separate button needed. Save is still what makes it survive a reload.

Ball Drop lens

The ball carries a warped crop of your camera rather than a flat one, so the face fills the sphere instead of sitting small in the middle of the room. Power is how hard the centre bulges — 1.0 is no distortion, higher pushes the nose into the glass. Crop is how much around your head to take: lower is tighter. Both need head tracking, since the crop centres on the tracked face.

Signature lifetimes

How long a name stays on the signature registry. Takeover and Legend are session-scoped and have no clock — they clear when the dock's session rotates. Note the supporter wall deliberately ignores all of this: bricks are permanent until the wall wraps, because a brick cannot vanish out of the middle of a physical wall.

Preview

Plays an effect on the live overlay through the identical code path as a real spin — but lays no brick and is never persisted. Safe to use mid-stream if you want to see one; it will appear on stream, so pick your moment.

State

Live from the dock. The wall rebuilds from this on every overlay reload.
