Tier floors

In coin equivalents — 1 TikTok coin = 1 YouTube jewel = $0.01 of viewer spend, and a Super Chat converts at the same rate. A gift below the spin floor produces no spin at all: that floor doubles as the qualification threshold. Floors must be strictly increasing.

Enabled results

Disabled results are never selected and never shown; probability redistributes equally among the rest. At least one must stay on. With exactly one enabled the overlay skips the spin animation — a reel that can only land on one slice is a lie. Freeze & Shatter and Snapshot Echo use the Gesture Engine's captured stream; keep that window open for their interactive versions. Both still have a safe fallback when tracking is unavailable.

Effect longevity

How long each tier's effect HOLDS on screen, in milliseconds — the dwell between the impact landing and the effect clearing. This is the main dial for how much a spin interrupts you: the same effect reads as a flourish or as a takeover purely on duration. Applied when a spin is RESOLVED, so a spin always plays with the timing that was set when it fired.

Carousel coverage & position

The Center-Stage Rail always spans the full stream width so every enabled result stays visible. Rail height controls its total screen coverage. Y position is normalized to a percentage of the canvas, so it stays correct at every browser-source height (positive is down). Spin time controls the complete fast-to-slow carousel moment. The card under the marker grows as it passes, and Legendary chances are mixed through the rail as individual gold cards. The cards own the full rail; headline and winner copy sit above it. Card scale adjusts only the icons and labels inside. Changes apply to the live overlay immediately as you type; Save makes them survive a reload.

Carousel copy

Edit the words viewers see during the spin. Use {result} in either winner template to insert the selected option name. Leave any box blank to hide that copy completely. Changes preview live and save locally in both the control and overlay, so they survive browser-source reloads.

Option names

Ball Drop lens

The ball carries a warped crop of your camera rather than a flat one, so the face fills the sphere instead of sitting small in the middle of the room. Power is how hard the centre bulges — 1.0 is no distortion, higher pushes the nose into the glass. Crop is how much around your head to take: lower is tighter. Both need head tracking, since the crop centres on the tracked face.

Signature lifetimes

How long a name stays on the signature registry. Takeover and Legend are session-scoped and have no clock — they clear when the dock's session rotates. Note the supporter wall deliberately ignores all of this: bricks are permanent until the wall wraps, because a brick cannot vanish out of the middle of a physical wall.

Wheel tester

Test full wheel spin shows the real spinning rail, lands on your selected result, and then plays its effect. Preview result only skips directly to the effect. Neither test lays a brick or persists, but both appear on the live overlay.
