Gold-Pass Chance Model

Each block is one complete pass containing every enabled result once. Purple passes land at the gift’s normal tier; gold passes upgrade whichever result wins to Legendary. The gift amount sets the minimum number of gold passes, and the operator can tune the exact floors.

SPIN FLOORany qualifying gift
1 of 8 gold passes · Legendary is possible for everyone.
POWER FLOORhigher gift amount
2 of 8 gold passes · more gold placements appear.
TAKEOVER FLOORhigher gift amount
4 of 8 gold passes · gold and purple are equally present.
LEGEND FLOORhighest gift amount
6 of 8 gold passes · favored, dramatic, but still a watched chance.

Why full passes: every pass contains the same enabled outcomes, so adding gold placements raises only the Legendary upgrade chance. It does not quietly make Spotlight, Stamp, Ball Drop, or any other result more likely.
